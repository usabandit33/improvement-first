Questions people ask when the septic permit line looks like one number

What is the difference between a Wake County Improvement Permit and a Construction Authorization?

The Improvement Permit is the soil and site evaluation. The Construction Authorization is the design-and-install approval. Wake County publishes them as two fees on the wastewater table.

Where are Wake County septic fees published?

Onsite Water Protection wastewater fees sit on wake.gov. The Board of Commissioners can change the schedule. Confirm the current table for your lot. This site does not invent a dollar figure.

Should I pay the Construction Authorization before the soil evaluation?

The Construction Authorization follows an approved Improvement Permit. Ask if the soil work is finished before you pay the second county fee. This page is not legal advice.

Do towns inside Wake County use the same table?

Unincorporated Wake is the county wastewater table. Confirm jurisdiction. Durham County next door publishes different Improvement Permit and Construction Authorization amounts.

What is the hybrid or private evaluator path?

State law lets a licensed soil scientist, authorized onsite wastewater evaluator, or professional engineer do some of the work. Wake County publishes a reduced hybrid fee as a share of the standard table. Ask which path is on the application.

What should be in writing?

Improvement Permit fee, Construction Authorization fee, design-review add-on if any, who files, and any separate installer charge — before you sign. Raleigh phone numbers are listed so you can ask the shop the same questions.
