Wake County bills the soil eval and the build permit as two fees. Ask which one is on your quote.

Raleigh-area homeowners on septic often see one “permit” line. Wake County Onsite Water Protection publishes an Improvement Permit and a Construction Authorization as separate charges. Use this list before you pay.

  • Look up the current Wake County wastewater fee table on wake.gov — Improvement Permit and Construction Authorization are separate lines.
  • Ask the installer which line is on the quote: IP, CA, design review, or installer time.
  • Do not pay the Construction Authorization before the soil evaluation that supports the Improvement Permit is done.
  • Name the jurisdiction. Unincorporated Wake, a town, and Durham County are not the same table.
  • Ask whether you are on the county path or a hybrid / private evaluator path (fees differ by statute).
  • Call a shop that publishes a phone on its own website and run through this list.
